# About Me

I am a PhD student at the [RAND School of Public Policy](https://www.rand.edu){:target="_blank"} and a Non-Resident Fellow at [IFP](https://ifp.org){:target="_blank"} based in Washington, DC. I focus on artificial intelligence and American national security.

At RAND, I work with [CAST](https://www.rand.org/global-and-emerging-risks/centers/ai-security-and-technology.html){:target="_blank"} to advise government agencies on frontier AI deployment, developing policy options and technical measures to advance reliability and security. 

At IFP, I work on [The Launch Sequence](https://ifp.org/launch){:target="_blank"} to develop field strategy and project proposals for AI resilience.

Previously, I graduated with a BS and an MS in computer science from Georgia Tech. I interned as a data scientist at the Georgia Tech Research Institute, Federal Reserve Board, and Washington Post.
